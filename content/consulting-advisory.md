I have worked in the Open Source community for over 25 years and in the Open Source industry for almost 20.
In particular, I learned a lot as a founding engineer at Grafana Labs, an open core business which I helped grow from 0 to unicorn, while aligning with healthy open source projects and communities.

Now, I advise startups (especially seed to Series B) and founders exploring open source business models.

I can help with:

* Choosing an Open Source business model that fits your product and stage
* Making clear licensing decisions that protect your business and your community
* Turning your project into products, supporting GTM (Go-To-Market) in harmony with engineering
* Building a healthy community of users and contributors
* Strengthening internal culture and operating practices as you scale
* Preparing for fundraising, and knowing when not to
* Surfacing blind spots and risks
* Choosing future-proof technology and architecture

Reach out on LinkedIn or by email (first name + last name at gmail) if you're working through one of these challenges.
